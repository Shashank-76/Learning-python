python is a high level, general purpose programming language created by Guido van Rossum in 1991.

High level means it's closer to human language than machine language — you write print("hello") instead of binary or complex commands.

General purpose means you can use it for almost anything — web development, data science, AI, automation, games etc.

What makes Python special:

Simple syntax — reads almost like English
Beginner friendly — less strict than other languages
Huge community — tons of libraries and support
Versatile — used everywhere from startups to NASA
